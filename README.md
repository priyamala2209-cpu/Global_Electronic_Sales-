## 📊 Global Electronic Sales - Sales Analysis Master Project
This analysis was performed on the Global Retail Sales dataset covering multiple countries, currencies, and product categories. The objective was to understand customer segmentation, operational efficiency (delivery), and financial impact of currency/country.


## 📌 Project Overview
This project provides a comprehensive analysis of global electronic sales data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends. The analysis covers data cleaning, transformation, visualization, KPI tracking, and revenue forecasting to support data-driven business decisions.

The dataset contains sales transactions across different regions, products, and time periods, analyzed using both Excel and Python to generate actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Objective
- To clean and transform raw sales data into an analysis-ready format
- To analyze sales performance across products, regions, and time
- To build an interactive dashboard for KPI tracking
- To identify key business trends, seasonal patterns, and revenue drivers
- To forecast future revenue and provide strategic recommendations

## 🔄 Workflow
1.  **Data Collection:** Imported raw global electronic sales dataset (CSV/Excel)
2.  **Data Cleaning & Transformation:** Handled missing values, duplicates, incorrect formats using Power Query and Pandas
3.  **Data Validation:** Checked data types, date formats, and sales calculations
4.  **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Analyzed sales distribution, trends, and correlations
5.  **Data Visualization:** Created charts for product performance, regional sales, and seasonal trends
6.  **Dashboard Creation:** Built interactive Power BI dashboard with DAX measures
7.  **KPI Calculation:** Tracked Total Revenue, Profit, Sales Quantity, AOV, etc.
8.  **Forecasting & Insights:** Performed revenue forecasting and derived business insights

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Revenue:** `SUM(Sales Amount)` - Overall business performance
- **Total Profit:** `Revenue - Cost`
- **Total Orders / Quantity Sold:** Number of products sold
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** `Total Revenue / Total Orders`
- **Profit Margin:** `(Total Profit / Total Revenue) * 100`
- **Top Selling Product / Category:** By Revenue & Quantity
- **Regional Performance:** Sales by Country / Region
- **Monthly / Quarterly Growth:** Trend analysis

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- **Sales by Product Category:** Identified highest and lowest performing products
- **Sales by Region:** Compared performance across countries/regions
- **Time Series Analysis:** Analyzed monthly, quarterly, and yearly sales trends
- **Customer Analysis:** Purchase frequency and buying patterns
- **Correlation Analysis:** Relationship between discount, quantity, and profit
- **Seasonality Check:** Peak sales periods and low seasons

**Key Plots Used:**
- Bar Chart – Product-wise Sales
- Line Chart – Monthly Revenue Trend
- Pie Chart – Regional Sales Share
- Heatmap – Correlation Analysis
- Scatter Plot – Discount vs Profit

## 📊 Revenue Forecasting
- Applied time series analysis to historical sales data
- Used moving averages and trend lines to predict future sales
- Forecasted next quarter revenue based on past trends and seasonality
- Visualized actual vs. forecasted revenue using Plotly

> Forecasting helps in inventory planning, budget allocation, and strategic decision making.

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- Top 3 products contributed to 65%+ of total revenue
- North America and Europe are the highest revenue-generating regions
- Q4 (Oct-Dec) showed peak sales due to festive season and promotions
- High discounts led to increased quantity sold but lower profit margin
- Repeat customers drive higher AOV compared to new customers

## ✅ Business Recommendations
1.  Focus marketing efforts on top-selling products and high-performing regions
2.  Optimize pricing strategy – reduce excessive discounts to improve profit margin
3.  Increase stock for peak season  to avoid stock-outs
4.  Launch loyalty programs to increase repeat customer purchases
5.  Expand sales in low-performing regions with targeted promotions
6.  Implement automated reporting dashboard for real-time KPI monitoring
